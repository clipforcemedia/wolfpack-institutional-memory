## 1. Product boundary and user roles
- Public visitor: browse Home, Brotherhood, Library preview, Store, Cohort; view three free library samples; purchase store products as guest.
- Registered unpaid user: account settings, public pages and store; no paid library/community entitlement.
- Paid Brotherhood member: above plus paid library downloads, asynchronous community, member dashboard and billing management; entitlement lasts through paid period after cancellation.
- Moderator/admin: approve content, manage discussions/reports, respond to support, restore eligible purchase downloads, reconcile giving and orders. Privileged functions server-only with audit logs.
- Guest purchaser: one-time purchase with email and secure order recovery; does not need membership or account.
- Cohort visitor: consented email/interest form only. No marketplace operations.

## 2. Information architecture
Five primary navigation destinations: Home, Brotherhood, Resource Library, Digital Store, The Cohort.
Necessary subordinate routes: /login, /signup, /verify, /reset-password, /dashboard, /account, /account/delete, /billing, /community, /store/success, /store/recover, /legal/terms, /legal/privacy, /legal/refunds, /giving and /support (support email instructions only). Route names are implementation suggestions, not new navigation destinations.
- Home: mission hero; Brotherhood, library and store previews; Cohort Coming Soon; legally reviewed giving explanation; dominant “Join The Brotherhood — $5/Month” CTA.
- Brotherhood: $5/month benefits, recurring terms, signup, cancellation clarity.
- Library: five categories, metadata title/keyword search, category filter, 20 written items (four/category; three free) and two interactive tools; private downloads.
- Store: 20-product grid (10 practical, 10 creative); $3 or $5; thumbnail, concise contents/format/license/refund disclosure, purchase button; no cart, reviews, advanced filters or dedicated PDPs.
- Cohort: Coming Soon, five future category summaries, email + intent (offer/hire/both) + primary category; clear consent, unsubscribe.
- Community: simple paid-member threads and replies, reporting, moderator hide/delete; no chat/DMs/likes/gamification.
- Dashboard: status, shortcuts, recent discussions, account and billing.

## 3. Architecture and trust boundaries
Frontend: Lovable-generated responsive web application. Backend: Supabase Auth, Postgres with Row Level Security, private Storage, server-side functions; Stripe-hosted Checkout and Billing Portal; tested transactional email provider. Choose only supported runtime integration patterns during implementation.
- Browser is untrusted: no Stripe secret, service-role key, private storage path grants, admin actions or payment-state authority in client code.
- Stripe verified webhooks are source of truth for successful subscription and one-time payment transitions; process idempotently using unique event IDs and transaction boundaries.
- Supabase Postgres records app-level access; RLS denies by default and is tested with anonymous, guest, member, other-member and admin identities.
- Private Storage never exposes permanent public URLs; server validates entitlement/purchase and issues expiring grants.
- Stripe Checkout success/cancel redirects are display states only; a redirect never activates membership or releases paid files.
- Secrets stored only in secure environment; separate test and production credentials, explicit webhook endpoint verification.
- All tables have stable UUID primary keys, created_at/updated_at as relevant, unique constraints for external IDs, appropriate foreign keys and indexes. Exact SQL deferred until implementation and verified against current platform APIs.

## 5. Billing and entitlement state machines
### Subscription
- Signup: managed email/password registration; create Stripe-hosted subscription checkout; verify email and payment; grant entitlement only after verified successful payment and verified identity.
- Renewal: webhook verified; update paid-through timestamp from authoritative Stripe subscription/invoice state.
- Cancel: customer uses provider-hosted billing portal or verified server action; cancellation stops future renewals and keeps entitlement through paid-through date.
- Initial payment failure: no entitlement. Renewal failure: explicit policy REQUIRED before build (recommended: retain only through already-paid period; do not invent a grace period).
- Refund/chargeback: revoke or adjust entitlement per verified status and consumer policy; record giving reversal.
- Duplicate, delayed and out-of-order events: idempotent handler; reconcile Stripe current state before destructive transitions; periodic consistency check.
- Account deletion: reauthenticate, explicitly confirm, cancel subscription first, verify cancellation, revoke sessions/entitlements, delete/anonymize eligible PII while retaining required accounting data. Partial failure blocks irreversible deletion and escalates to support.

### Digital order and fulfillment
- Guest selects published $3/$5 product; hosted one-time Checkout collects email; checkout metadata references server-side order/product ID, not trusted client price.
- Only server-verified successful paid event marks order paid. Price and product IDs validated server-side.
- Success page polls/retrieves server-verified paid state and presents download. Transactional email sends a secure purchase-recovery link.
- Private Supabase Storage serves files through server-verified, short-lived authorized grants.
- **Five downloads per purchased item (D027 amendment):** enforce cap atomically in database, including concurrent requests. Exact counting semantics (e.g. signed grant issued vs completed file transfer) must be chosen and tested before build; avoid counting failed transfers as completed if feasible. Support can restore access after verifying purchase. Grant expiration and token replay are controlled.
- Guest link must not expose a permanent file URL; forwarded links have limited lifetime and use; rate-limit recovery and verify email ownership.
- Refund/chargeback updates order status and revokes future grants according to law and published policy; retain required records.
- Test PDFs, images and ZIP bundles on mobile and desktop; never fulfill solely from success URL.

## 6. Content and operations
- Resource launch inventory: 20 written (four per each of five categories, including exactly three public samples) + 2 simple tools. Human approval and claim verification mandatory.
- Store launch inventory: 20 products (10 practical, 10 creative); only PDF/PNG/JPG/ZIP; personal-use rights verified; product/file formats and license visible before payment.
- Four new resources/month is internal target, not guaranteed entitlement.
- Discussion moderation minimum: rules, reporting, admin review/hide/remove, spam/rate limits, basic incident escalation. No private messaging.
- Support: dedicated @forfathers.com inbox; internal 24-calendar-hour first-response target; support email on receipts and relevant pages. Member suggestion form and Cohort interest form are not support channels.
- Giving: $1 per qualifying successful $5 membership payment in segregated ledger (not necessarily segregated bank funds); reversals for refunds/chargebacks; quarterly transfer to verified 501(c)(3) and public report of actual disbursement, after legal review. No claim of buyer tax deductibility.
- Analytics: aggregate visits, signups, cancellations, active paid members, MRR excluding one-time store sales, store sales and payment failures. Avoid behavioral profiling.

## 7. Security and privacy minimum
- Managed Auth with verification, secure reset, abuse rate limits, protected sessions.
- RLS authorization for every sensitive table and paid resource, server-side role checks for admin/moderation.
- Webhook signature validation, event deduplication, retries, safe logging and alerting.
- Private object storage, expiring grants, no service-role credentials in browser.
- Least-privilege secret management; backup/restore drill; production monitoring.
- Consent, unsubscribe, retention and deletion flows for interest emails; privacy/terms/refund disclosures before live operations.
- WCAG 2.2 AA target, with actual keyboard, focus, contrast, forms, touch and screen-reader checks; no unsupported conformance claims.

## 8. Implementation sequence — one validated slice at a time
0. **Prebuild decisions/verification:** resolve exact download-count semantics and failed-renewal access policy; verify current Lovable/Supabase/Stripe integration approach; establish entity/legal/tax plan. No production checkout.
1. Scaffold mobile-first public pages and design tokens; stub data only. Acceptance: five nav destinations, CTA, accessibility baseline, Coming Soon explicit, no real payment links.
2. Configure Supabase project, managed auth and RLS; implement public samples, library metadata and paid gating. Acceptance: anonymous users cannot read private assets; member entitlement enforced server-side.
3. Implement Stripe test-mode subscription checkout, verified webhooks, paid-through access, cancellation and billing portal. Acceptance: successful, failed, duplicate and out-of-order events tested; no access from redirect alone.
4. Implement test-mode guest store checkout, private storage, five-download counter, transactional email and recovery. Acceptance: paid-only download, 5/5 allowed, 6th denied, concurrent bypass blocked, refund revokes.
5. Implement simple members-only community, moderation and dashboard. Acceptance: nonmembers cannot read/write; reporting/removal and spam controls work.
6. Publish approved content inventory, Cohort interest form, giving ledger/reporting, legal/support pages and minimal analytics. Acceptance: correct inventory counts, consent/unsubscribe, verified ledger totals.
7. End-to-end security/accessibility/mobile/backup and financial reconciliation; complete D054 checklist; authorize production separately.

## 9. Acceptance tests (minimum, executable as manual scripts until automated)
T01 Anonymous can view three samples but cannot download a paid resource.
T02 Unpaid registered user cannot access community or paid resources.
T03 Member with verified email and successful payment gains entitlement; success URL alone does not.
T04 Duplicate Stripe event does not double grant access, create orders or allocate Giving Fund reserve.
T05 Failed initial subscription payment grants no access; renewal failure follows documented policy.
T06 Cancellation prevents renewal and access remains until paid-through date, then expires.
T07 Guest pays $3/$5 and receives immediate authorized download plus working email recovery.
T08 Unpaid/forged order cannot access private product; leaked storage key cannot bypass auth.
T09 Five successful downloads permitted; sixth denied; concurrent requests cannot exceed five.
T10 Refund/chargeback reconciles order/entitlement and Giving Fund ledger without double reversal.
T11 Account deletion cannot complete if subscription cancellation fails; eligible PII removed afterward.
T12 Member reports harmful thread; moderator can hide it; nonmember cannot read discussions.
T13 Cohort consent captured and unsubscribe works; no listings, payouts or transactions.
T14 $1 Giving reserve per qualifying payment and correct quarterly reconciliation/reporting.
T15 Mobile card/wallet paths (where available), forms, keyboard and screen reader tested.
T16 Backup restore and payment-webhook replay/recovery drill completed.

## 10. Explicit exclusions
No annual membership, free trial, store member discounts, cart, reviews, advanced search, AI apps, live chat, DMs, native mobile app, full Cohort marketplace, marketplace payouts, provider onboarding, international Cohort transactions, behavioral attribution or public SLA.

## 11. Open implementation validations (not new product decisions)
- Current Lovable/Supabase/Stripe integration and deployment compatibility, including webhook runtime.
- Counting semantics for five downloads and safe interrupted-transfer recovery.
- Failed-renewal/grace behavior and customer communication.
- Transactional email sender and domain DNS configuration.
- Product rights, content approval and moderation ownership.
- Entity/legacy Delaware LLC, Ohio formation, tax registrations, charitable promotion and consumer-law reviews.
- Specific 501(c)(3) recipient, transfer account/process and verifiable receipt.
- Exact schema, migrations and RLS policies to be created and tested in implementation.
