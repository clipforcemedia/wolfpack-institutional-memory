## Critical findings — resolve before implementation or live checkout
1. **D027 vs D046 delivery conflict (BLOCKER FOR SPEC).** D027 says five downloads per purchase only if provider-native, explicitly 'do not build custom download tracking'; D046 approves Stripe + Supabase custom secure file fulfillment and download counters. Stripe does not itself host/limit product files. Decision needed: authorize a minimal server-side atomic purchase-level counter and private storage, or revise the five-download requirement. Do not silently override D027.
2. **Stale register guardrails (DOCUMENTATION DEFECT).** The 'v0.1 Scope Guardrails' section incorrectly says annual membership undecided (D009 locked monthly only), charitable recipient/timing/refund adjustments undecided (D029–D034 resolved except specific recipient), and 'Current Decision Queue' and 'Execution Checklist' imply decisions remain open. Correct these without changing decisions.
3. **D040 signup / guest digital purchase / D052 deletion require distinct identity paths (IMPLEMENTATION BLOCKER).** Brotherhood requires verified account plus paid entitlement. One-time Digital Store guests must receive secure fulfillment and recover purchases without member accounts. Account deletion must cancel subscriptions without destroying legally retained transaction records or legitimate guest-order recovery. Define identity and order ownership model, webhook idempotency, and failed-checkout recovery.
4. **Giving Fund legal/ledger requirements (LAUNCH BLOCKER).** $1 of each qualifying $5 membership payment is a company reserve, not necessarily a charitable customer donation. Review state charitable-sales-promotion rules, donation language, refunds/chargebacks, quarterly ledger and chosen verified 501(c)(3) recipient before advertising or charging.
5. **Entity, Stripe eligibility, tax and consumer terms (LAUNCH BLOCKER).** Ohio entity/Delaware legacy status unresolved. Verify legal seller identity, bank/payment onboarding, product/subscription taxability and jurisdictions, auto-renew disclosures, cancellation, digital product remedies, privacy, and data retention before live checkout.
6. **Discussion community lacks explicit moderation and abuse SOP (LAUNCH BLOCKER).** D007 promises member-only asynchronous discussions. Need minimal threads/replies, reporting, rules, moderator removal/suspension, spam throttling, and escalation. Avoid DMs, live chat, reputation, or AI moderation until later.
7. **Email-only support vs forms is not a conflict.** D050 is customer support email only; D017 member resource suggestion form and D059 Cohort interest form are distinct non-support functions. Keep separate.
8. **D043 grid needs pre-purchase disclosure.** License, file types, contents, taxes/total and refund terms must be visible without requiring a full product-detail-page build.
9. **D013 + D021 inventory is a large production gate.** 20 written resources + 2 tools + 20 store products = 42 deliverables, plus community and commerce. This is approved scope but a schedule risk. Prepare content inventory and review checklist; reducing quantity requires explicit ADR.
10. **D048 failed-payment state undefined.** Specify entitlement behavior for initial payment failure, renewals, retries, grace periods, cancellation, chargebacks and delayed webhooks; verify Stripe Billing Portal and webhook events in test mode.
11. **D052 account deletion is irreversible.** Require reauthentication, explicit confirmation, verified cancellation-before-deletion, support fallback, data retention schedule and recovery from partial failures.
12. **D056 5% Cohort fee remains provisional.** No Cohort payouts, listings, provider onboarding or transactions in v0.1. D060 founder discretion authorizes only a future go/no-go decision, not build.

## Proposed v0.1 architecture (subject to D027 resolution)
- Frontend: Lovable-generated responsive web app; public Home, Brotherhood, Library preview, Store, Cohort interest page, plus necessary account/dashboard and legal routes.
- Auth/data: Supabase managed authentication, Postgres with RLS; separate member entitlements, digital orders, purchases, download grants, giving ledger, community moderation, and interest submissions.
- Payments: Stripe-hosted checkout for subscriptions and guest one-time products; Stripe Customer Portal for billing management where supported. Server-side verified and idempotent webhooks are authoritative. Never trust success redirect for entitlement.
- Files: private Supabase Storage; expiring signed links generated only after order verification. Download limit enforcement requires explicit D027 revision and concurrency-safe server-side counting. Email delivery via tested transactional sender.
- Community: members-only threads/replies, report and moderation functions; no private messaging.
- Analytics: aggregate traffic, verified Stripe revenue/subscription events, orders, failures; no advanced tracking.
- Cohort: Coming Soon description and minimal consented interest form only.
